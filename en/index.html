Kokelapa is a special fresh coconut from the best young coconut producing land in West Java, Indonesia

Kokelapa is elegantly presented, providing a more sophisticated and tantalizing coconut experience. White diamond cut coconuts are peeled using a special technique, creating a neat, diamond-like shape. Its premium appearance is not only aesthetically pleasing but also practical for serving in restaurants, cafes, hotels, special events, and personal consumption.

Advantages

  • More inclusive at affordable prices.

  • No preservatives.

  • Husk or coconut fiber does not turn yellow due to oxidation.

  • Even though the husk is peeled, the freshness of the water and fruit flesh is maintained.

  • Restaurant logo hot stamp customization available.

  • Suitable for formal or casual events because it is easy to open and can be consumed immediately.

  • Free shipping for Bandung area.

Storage & Shelf Life

  • Store in a cool place to maintain freshness.

  • Optimal shelf life is up to 7 days after peeling if stored properly.

  • Coconuts can still be consumed if stored in the refrigerator for more than 7 days, but their freshness will decrease.

Health Benefits of Coconut

  • Coconut water is rich in electrolytes to balance body fluids.

  • Natural sugars provide instant energy.

  • Rich in vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants.

  • Helps regulate blood pressure and supports cardiovascular health.

About Kokelapa

Founded in Bandung Indonesia in 2022, Kokelapa is a producer of diamond cut coconut driven by the spirit of collaboration, entrepreneurship, and empowerment. While not the first in its field, Kokelapa aspires to elevate the value of local agricultural products and deliver a refreshing, enjoyable coconut consumption experience. Kokelapa offers an inclusive approach at a more affordable price. With a commitment to quality and sustainability, Kokelapa strives to be a trusted partner in bringing innovation from Indonesia's natural resources to a wider market.
